PROJECT 03 3D / ANIMATION

3D SHOWREEL

BLENDER 2025
01 / 03

THE THEME

For this project, I needed to choose a theme and do a showreel video where I could show these 7 topics: intro, particles, physics, 3 animations, motion tracking, outro and breakdown. I chose Star Wars as a theme and did the "TEST OF STRENGTH" for Darth Vader.

So I started by modelling the environment and doing the animations. Due to the lack of time for the project I didn't model the character. Darth Vader and the droids were from Sketchfab, a website with many 3D models. I just did the paintwrap and rigging for the animations.

02 / 03

ANIMATING

After the environment was modeled and the rigging was done, I just needed to do 3 animations for Darth Vader: an idle, a walk cycle and another of my choice. I chose to do a Force animation, where he lifts a heavy door.

With the animations done I just needed to do the physics and the motion tracking. In the physics I chose to make him break glass and use throwable drones, where they get suspended in the air with the Force and then fall. For the motion tracking, the part that I liked the least, I went to a place close to my house and filmed it, so Darth Vader would appear there. As it was filmed with my phone, the quality wasn't the best.

03 / 03

ALL MERGED TOGETHER

And now, after hours of rendering and AMD crashes, I finally had the final version of this showreel.

I compiled all the videos in one final sequence and added a breakdown at the end. Here's the final result.
