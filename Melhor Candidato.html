MELHOR CANDIDATO

DAVINCI RESOLVE 2024
01 / 03

FIRST IDEAS

For this project we needed to create a short film from scratch, create a storyboard and a script, do the location scouting and get the actors. We started with a little request for actors on social media and got an adult male and an adult female. Our final idea was to make a small comedy: the worst possible candidate competes in the presidential elections and, from night to day, out of nowhere, he wins. Now he needs to train with his assistant to be able to do a good job.

02 / 03

ON THE PAPER

Now that the idea was on point we just needed to do the production plan, the breakdown, the service sheet and the casting sheet. After all that we just needed to get the actors and start filming. This film was shot in Cascais, at the Palácio dos Condes de Castro Guimarães, and at the nearby beach.
