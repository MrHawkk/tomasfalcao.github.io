PROJECT 01 3D / MODELLING

PORTAL GUN MODELLING

BLENDER 2024
01 / 03

FIRST STEPS

After choosing the Portal Gun as a model, I needed to find a blueprint that had all the angles of the model, so it would be easier to model it.

After finding the blueprint, I started modeling the gun in Blender, using the blueprint as a reference.

02 / 03

LIFE WITH COLORS

Now I needed to give it life with colors, so I started by texturizing the Portal Gun with the help of the Blender community.

After coloring and texturizing it, it was time for the lighting.

I used three-point lighting with a key light, a fill light and a back light to give it a more realistic look.

03 / 03

FINAL RESULT

And now, after hours of rendering and AMD crashes, I finally had the final version of this Portal Gun.

I decided to experiment with different materials and create three different versions of the final model.
